# Blog Topics and Clusters: Konsilience and Trasys

## How to use this file

**What a cluster is:** one big guide on a topic (the pillar), plus 6-8 smaller posts that each answer one question, all linked to each other and to the matching service page. Google then treats us as the specialist on that topic.

**How to start:** do not write everything. Pick **2 clusters for Konsilience and 1 for Trasys**, finish those (pillar plus 4-6 supporting posts), then add more.

**Before writing any post:**
1. Check the real search demand in Google Search Console, Google Keyword Planner and "People also ask" for the topic. Treat the keyword ideas below as starting points, not proven volumes.
2. Add something original: our own numbers, screenshots from our 35 projects, real timelines, comparison tables. Anything cost-related must come from our own quotes and experience, with the date.
3. Put a direct answer in the first 100 words, use question-style headings, link the sources, and finish with one CTA.
4. Run the originality checks from the website brief before publishing.

**Cadence:** Konsilience 1 post per week. Trasys 1 post every 2 weeks. Review every post after 90 days and update it.
